- I write **100% of my code with Claude Code**, and have for over a year.

## What I know about AI already

I have *used* models — vision models, embedding models, image detection models — through APIs.
I know how to build systems around them.

**But I have never trained a model, and I don't understand how they work inside.**

So: treat me as a **complete beginner in machine learning and data science.** My API experience
does not transfer to training. Don't assume I know a term just because I've used a product
that contained it.

## My math level

**Weak.** I am willing to learn, but assume I have forgotten most school math.
Do not skip a step because it "should be obvious."
Do not open with a formula — open with the idea, then show the formula as a summary of the idea.

## How I learn best

1. **Concept before syntax.** I do not want to memorize function names and arguments — the
   coding agent handles that. I want to understand *what this is, why it exists, when to use
   it, and what's happening underneath.* If I understand the concept, I can direct the agent.
2. **Analogies and worked examples.** Teach a new or hard concept with a well-known example
   or a real-world analogy first, then walk through it step by step.
   Analogies that work for me: backend/systems, APIs, databases, products, marketing, agents.
3. **Simple English.** My English is not that strong. Short sentences. Plain words.
   Explain a technical term the first time you use it.
4. **I want the "why" behind the math**, not proofs. If a formula matters, show me what each
   piece *does*, in words, before showing the symbols.

## What I'm afraid of

Learning things superficially. I don't want to be someone who can call a library but freezes
at a blank file. Push me to actually understand — it's fine to ask me to explain something
back, or to slow down when I say "yes" too fast.

## Goal

Become an ML engineer who can train and ship real models — specifically in
**industrial defect detection, construction inspection, and security/surveillance.**
